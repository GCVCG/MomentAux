\section{The law's components}\label{app:law}

\paragraph{Feature-gain curves.} $G$ is non-monotone in data and
dataset-specific in shape: CIFAR-10 $4.81/5.52/3.97/0.65$ at
$0.5/1/2.5/5$k images; CIFAR-100 $3.88/5.33/6.35/3.66$; Tiny-ImageNet
falls monotonically $4.33/2.67/1.70$ ($1/5/10$k); STL-10
$4.70/4.98/3.20$. Wherever the readout term is flat, the end-to-end
envelope peak coincides with the $G$ peak -- the envelope's shape
\emph{is} the feature-gain curve.

\paragraph{The readout branch.} Mapped on ceiling-free CIFAR-10:
$+1.80$ at baseline $39.4$, $+1.14$ at $51.7$, $+0.44$ at $69.1$ and at
$80.7$ -- positive throughout and decaying, never growing, toward
sufficiency. Below the crossing, measured values reach $-2.8$ (base
$8.9$) and $-2.7$ (base $5.3$). The crossing bracket $[31.8, 40.3]$ is
pinned by a Swin cell still negative at $31.8$ and a conv cell already
positive at $40.3$.

\paragraph{Label-space crossings.} On byte-identical CIFAR-100 pixels, a
full $2{\times}2$ of training label space $\times$ probe label space
(100-way vs.\ 20-way) moves $G$ by at most $1\sigma$ -- invariance. On
Tiny-ImageNet the same crossing finds two real, opposing effects
(probe-space $+1.9$, checkpoint-set $+1.6$, both ${\sim}5\sigma$) that
cancel on the diagonal; $G$ comparisons in the paper therefore always fix
the probe space.

\section{Selected controls}\label{app:controls}

\paragraph{Contamination.} Re-evaluating the low-data CIFAR-100 cells on
ciFAIR-100 \citep{barz2020cifair}, which replaces the test set's 927
near-duplicates, moves the prior's gain by at most $0.20$ points at any
fraction -- both arms eat identical contamination, so it cancels in
$\Delta$.

\paragraph{Granularity, decisively.} Relabelling CIFAR-100's committed
subsets with their 20 coarse labels (byte-identical pixels, identical
steps) reproduces the full gain at matched data ($+5.84$ vs.\ $+5.30$ at
5\%) -- gain follows data and task performance, not class count. The
mirrored Tiny-ImageNet control (positional, visually incoherent coarse
groups) is the cell that falsified our own earlier account: class count
dropped tenfold, baseline accuracy did not rise, and no readout boost
appeared -- exactly as Eq.~\ref{eq:law} requires and as ``label count
drives gain'' does not.

\paragraph{Robustness.} On CIFAR-100-C \citep{hendrycks2019benchmarking},
corruption robustness tracks $55$--$60\%$ of the clean gain at low data
and is neutral at 100\% -- the prior neither buys nor costs robustness
beyond its accuracy effect.

\section{Domain notes}\label{app:domains}

EuroSAT: the prior beats SimCLR at 7 of 11 fractions (materially
$+0.8$--$+1.9$ in the 1--10\% band) -- consistent with SimCLR's
photographic view-invariances transferring poorly to satellite
statistics while the spectral target is domain-agnostic. DTD (texture)
is the reverse: SimCLR dominates from a few hundred images. Food-101 is
SSL territory on conv backbones. PathMNIST declines with added data for
\emph{every} method above ${\sim}10$--$15\%$ (baseline $92.0 \to 86.7$
from 10\% to 100\%), the signature of its centre-shifted test split;
we therefore interpret only its low-data cells. The domain axis thus
does not split ``photo vs.\ non-photo''; each domain's statistics decide
which currency is scarce.

\section{Reproducibility}\label{app:repro}

One entry point trains any cell (\texttt{train.py --config <cell> --seed
N}); one command regenerates every table from run records; one command
re-runs the sign-law audit. Subset indices, filter-bank fingerprints, and
per-run environment records (library versions, worker counts) are
committed. Training refuses to overwrite a completed cell, holds an
exclusive per-run lock (two of this study's incident classes made these
guards necessary), and writes checkpoints atomically; mirrors are
verified by \emph{loading and evaluating} checkpoints against their
recorded accuracies, not by file listing. The full harness -- configs,
subsets, banks, runner, aggregation, audit -- is released with the paper.

\section{Explainability probes}\label{app:explain}

The prior's mechanism is visible directly in the learned features
(Fig.~\ref{fig:explain}, CIFAR-100 @5\% champion pair). Class-activation
maps of the prior arm concentrate on object contours and texture
boundaries where the baseline's maps at this data scale are diffuse;
tapped-stage feature energy shows the same contrast; and t-SNE of
penultimate features shows visibly tighter class structure -- the
qualitative face of the $+6.3$-point probe gap at this cell.
Quantitatively, the cosine alignment between tapped features and the
bank's oriented-energy targets is $0.53$ for the prior arm against
$0.22$ for the baseline: the auxiliary loss leaves a measurable spectral
imprint on the representation even though its weight has decayed to zero
by the end of training.
