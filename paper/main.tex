\begin{abstract}
% DRAFT, one paragraph, <=250 words, no references, fusion vocabulary first.
Fusing prior knowledge with data-driven learning is attractive precisely
where data is scarce, yet no controlled account says when such fusion helps,
when it is redundant, and when it harms. We present a
benchmark that measures one fixed, hand-crafted source of knowledge, a
pinned bank of Gabor and moment spectral targets, injected only during training
through an auxiliary regression head at $\sim$2\% compute overhead, against
the leading data-driven alternatives (self-supervised learning with
SimCLR, SimSiam and DINO, ImageNet transfer, heavy augmentation, and
learned teachers) under one frozen recipe with
committed data subsets: 14 datasets, 7 backbone families, data scales from
500 to 1.28M images, resolutions from 32 to 224\,px, and models from 5.7M to
86M parameters ($2{,}800$ experimental cells; ${\sim}8{,}900$ training runs). The grid is organized by a
single empirical law, $\Delta = G + \mathrm{readout}(\mathrm{base})$,
separating each intervention's feature-level gain $G$ from a readout term
depending only on baseline accuracy. Registered in advance, the law
called the feature gain of unseen backbone families and of ImageNet-scale
cells from baseline heights alone (96\% of resolvable cells; $|\Delta-G|
\le 1.1$ points on five of six ImageNet-scale pairs). It yields
a sharp fusion taxonomy: sources supplying different currencies stack (prior
with augmentation), same-currency sources substitute (the prior against
effective self-supervised pre-training), and fusing into an already-informed
initialization taxes in proportion to what that initialization carries
(ImageNet transfer). A decision guide maps regime to the cheapest
intervention. Harness, configurations, subset indices, per-run records and
result tables are released at
\href{https://github.com/GCVCG/MomentAux}{\texttt{github.com/\allowbreak GCVCG/MomentAux}}.
\end{abstract}

\item A 2,800-cell, cost-normalized benchmark of data-efficiency interventions
\item One law, $\Delta=G+$readout(base), predicts fusion gains before training
\item Different-currency fusions stack; same-currency fusions substitute
\item Free spectral prior beats 2$\times$-compute SSL on ViTs under modern recipes
\item Attention's feature deficit persists at 1.28M images and grows with scale

\section*{Data availability}
All artifacts needed to reproduce every number in this paper are released
at \url{https://github.com/GCVCG/MomentAux}: the training and
evaluation harness (configs for all 2{,}800 cells, the committed subset
indices, the fingerprinted filter banks, the single training entry point,
the aggregation and audit scripts), and the complete result tables in open
formats. The tagged release additionally carries the \emph{raw} evidence as
downloadable assets: every run's \texttt{final.json} record (configuration
as executed, accuracy, parameter and floating-point-operation accounting,
environment), every linear-probe record, every per-epoch training curve, and
the campaign logs, with \texttt{SHA256} checksums. The latter comprise a per-cell
table (\texttt{all\_results.csv}: 2{,}800 rows with dataset, backbone,
intervention, fraction, seed count, accuracy mean and standard deviation,
probe accuracy, paired baseline, $\Delta$, its standard error, $G$ and
readout), a configuration-by-fraction view
(\texttt{results\_by\_portion.csv}), and a workbook combining both with a
column dictionary and the law audit. Every table and figure in the paper
regenerates from these files by one command, and the sign-law audit of
Table~\ref{tab:audit} is itself a released script rather than a manual
query. Public datasets are cited in Section~\ref{sec:datasets}; the
subset indices we release make the exact image selection reproducible
without redistributing the images. Model checkpoints (223\,GB) are not
distributed; every cell retrains from its committed configuration.
