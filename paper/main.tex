\begin{abstract}
% DRAFT -- one paragraph, <=250 words, no references, fusion vocabulary first.
Fusing prior knowledge with data-driven learning is attractive precisely
where data is scarce, yet the field lacks a controlled account of when such
fusion helps, when it is redundant, and when it actively harms. We present a
benchmark that measures one fixed, hand-crafted source of knowledge -- a
pinned bank of Gabor/moment spectral targets, injected only during training
through an auxiliary regression head at $\sim$2\% compute overhead -- against
the leading data-driven alternatives (SimCLR, SimSiam, DINO, ImageNet
transfer, heavy augmentation, learned teachers) under one frozen recipe with
committed data subsets: 14 datasets, 7 backbone families, data scales from
500 to 1.28M images, resolutions from 32 to 224\,px, and models from 5.7M to
86M parameters ($2{,}800$ experimental cells; ${sim}8{,}900$ training runs). The grid is organized by a
single empirical law, $\Delta = G + \mathrm{readout}(\mathrm{base})$,
separating each intervention's feature-level gain $G$ from a readout term
that depends only on baseline accuracy. The law is predictive: registered in
advance, it called the feature gain of unseen backbone families and of
ImageNet-scale cells from baseline heights alone (96\% of resolvable cells;
$|\Delta-G| \le 1.1$ points on five of six ImageNet-scale pairs). It yields
a sharp fusion taxonomy: sources fusing DIFFERENT currencies stack (prior +
augmentation), same-currency sources substitute (prior vs.\ effective SSL),
and fusing into an already-informed initialization taxes in proportion to
what the initialization carries (ImageNet transfer). A decision guide maps
regime to the cheapest effective intervention.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
% 3-5 bullets, <=85 chars each INCLUDING spaces (Elsevier limit).
\item A 2,800-cell, cost-normalized benchmark of data-efficiency interventions
\item One law, $\Delta=G+$readout(base), predicts fusion gains before training
\item Different-currency fusions stack; same-currency fusions substitute
\item Free spectral prior beats 2$\times$-compute SSL on ViTs under modern recipes
\item Attention's feature deficit persists at 1.28M images and grows with scale
\end{highlights}
